MORE THAN A CLUB · WENCHI HERITAGE

2012

Founded in Wenchi

Established with the foundational motto "... agya na ƆwƆ tumi", creating a sporting beacon for youth empowerment across the Bono, Bono East, and Ahafo regions.

GPL Promotion Ascent
