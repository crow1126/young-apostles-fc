Who We Are

Pride of Wenchi,
Heart of Bono

Young Apostles Football Club is a professional football club representing Wenchi and the wider Bono Region on Ghana's biggest stage — with the motto agya na ƆwƆ tumi.

Team founded in 2012, competed in the 2012/2013 Second Division league in Brong Ahafo and finished 4th. In 2013/2014 the club placed first in the zonal league but narrowly failed to progress to the Division One League.

After years of consistency through the lower divisions — including topping the zonal league in 2017/2018 — Young Apostles secured historic promotion to the Ghana Premier League in 2024, marking Wenchi's triumphant entry into top-flight football.

Our Journey

From Grassroots to the GPL

A decade of determination that changed Wenchi football forever.

2012

Club Founded

Young Apostles FC is established in Wenchi, Bono Region, with a mission to develop youth football and compete at the highest levels.

2012/13

Second Division Debut

Finished fourth in the Brong Ahafo Second Division in our inaugural competitive season.

2017/18

Zonal Champions

Topped the zonal league and earned qualification for the National Division One League.

2024

GPL Promotion

Historic promotion to the Ghana Premier League — Wenchi joins the top flight alongside fellow Bono clubs Nsoatreman, Aduana Stars, and Bechem United.

2026/27

Third GPL Campaign

Entering our third consecutive Premier League season with renewed ambition — targeting stability, growth, and a push up the table.
