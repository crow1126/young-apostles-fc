About Young Apostles FC

Founded in 2012 in Wenchi, Bono Region, Ghana, Young Apostles Football Club stands as the proud heartbeat of regional football excellence. Guided by the revered motto "AGYA NA ƆWƆ TUMI" (God Has Power), the club has risen from grassroots football to the prestigious Ghana Premier League.

With our passionate home support at Wenchi Sports Stadium, world-class youth development pipelines, and disciplined tactical philosophy under Coach Abu Abdul-Hanan, Young Apostles FC is dedicated to nurturing world-class talents and delivering thrilling top-tier football.
