Club Identity

Pride of Wenchi, Spirit of Bono

A modern football institution built on grit, talent discovery, and unwavering community devotion.

Ghana Premier League Promoted 2024

Empowering the youth and elevating regional football to continental standards.

Founded in 2012 in Wenchi, Young Apostles Football Club has carved out a storied path through the tiers of Ghanaian football. What began as a community youth initiative has evolved into one of the country's most respected talent incubators and a formidable force in the Ghana Premier League.

Our defining club philosophy — captured in our creed "... agya na ƆwƆ tumi" — champions discipline, resilience, and tactical excellence. From scouting grassroots talent across the Bono, Bono East, and Ahafo regions to exporting elite players like Mathew Anim Cudjoe to European top flights, Apostles stands as a beacon of football development.

Our Journey

Milestones & Rise Through The Ranks

Tracing the defining moments that propelled Young Apostles from Division Two to the Premier League.

2012 – 2013
Inception

Foundation & Second Division Debut

Young Apostles FC is founded in Wenchi. In our debut 2012/2013 campaign in the Brong Ahafo Second Division, the team demonstrated early promise by finishing 4th.

2016 – 2017
Promotion

Zonal Champions & Division One Entry

Swept through the Zonal League and won all Middle League matches to achieve landmark qualification into the National Division One League.

2019 – 2022
Talent Hub

National Sensation & Global Transfers

Apostles' youth wonderkid Mathew Anim Cudjoe gained nationwide acclaim, starring on loan at Asante Kotoko and Legon Cities before completing a historic transfer to Dundee United in Scotland.

June 2024
Historic GPL Ascent

Promotion to the Ghana Premier League

Secured promotion in a dramatic playoff penalty shootout against Techiman Heroes at the Accra Sports Stadium, ushering Wenchi into top-flight Ghanaian football.

2026 / 2027
Present Era

Premier League Contenders

Solidifying our top-flight stature, developing next-gen talents, and establishing Wenchi Sports Stadium as an impregnable fortress.
